Knowledge extraction

Feynman-style diagnostic from general understanding to specifics. It does not teach. Demonstrated knowledge can satisfy lesson checkpoints, but must be explained again in the final assessment.

3

Map + checkpoints

Build a branching graph of fundamentals and dependencies while presenting one active mission at a time. Starts in parallel with extraction after Clarification is frozen.

4

Lesson opening

Merge the frozen scope, generated graph, and diagnostic evidence. Begin as soon as enough of both parallel paths is available.

5

Lesson conversation

Socratic questions surface and repair gaps. The exact behavior is a testable starting point, not a settled framework.

6

Final explanation

The learner explains the whole idea unaided. Targeted follow-ups expose gaps; gaps loop to Socratic repair, then a fresh explanation measures learning.
