Privacy

Early-access version · last updated September 22, 2026

Worldview is a learning app: you bring a question and talk it through with an AI tutor. This page explains, in plain language, what we keep, why, and who helps us run the service. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a securely stored password.
  • Your lessons: the topics you choose, the lesson maps and research sources prepared for you, the text of your conversations (typed, or transcribed from speech), your progress, and your Notes.
  • Usage records: how many lessons you start, how long voice sessions last, and what they cost us to run, so we can keep the service working and price it fairly.
  • On your device: the app saves copies of your lessons and settings in your browser so it works quickly and can recover after a lost connection.

Your voice

When you use voice, your audio is streamed to our speech and AI providers so they can hear you and reply. Worldview saves the text of the conversation, not a recording of your voice.

Who helps us run Worldview

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the app:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • OpenAI, Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude) generate lesson plans, research, tutoring replies and understanding checks. Your lesson content is sent to them for that purpose.
  • Deepgram and Cartesia turn speech into text and text into speech in some voice modes.

These providers process data on our behalf. Some keep request data for a limited time under their own policies, for example to detect abuse.

How we use it

To give you lessons, remember your progress, show your history, keep the service reliable and secure, and understand costs. We may look at anonymous totals (for example, how many lessons finish) to improve Worldview. We do not read individual conversations unless you send one to us, for example with a copied transcript when reporting a problem.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from the account screen: tap your account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything saved with it from our servers. Copies saved in your browser are removed from the device you delete from. On any other device you used, sign out or clear this site's data in the browser settings to remove its copy.

Age

During early access Worldview is for adults (18 and over).

Changes and questions

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app's What's New notes. Contact details will be listed here before paid plans launch.
